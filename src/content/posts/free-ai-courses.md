Five free courses that take you from understanding AI at work to building and fine-tuning your own LLM applications.

*AI learning resources are everywhere, and the hard part is no longer finding something to study. It’s knowing where to start. Whether you work in a pharmacy, a marketing team or a data role, the right course depends on where you are now and what you want to build.*

**I’ve listed five free courses, starting with the gentlest and moving towards the more technical. Each one has a “Best for” line, so you can skip straight to the one that fits.**





## **1. Introduction to AI for Work (DataCamp)**

This is the easiest way in. It explains what AI, machine learning, generative AI and large language models (LLMs) are, then looks at how they’re used in everyday work. You don’t need a technical background.

**Best for:** Students, managers, marketers and complete beginners

**Link:** [DataCamp, Introduction to AI for Work](https://www.datacamp.com/courses/introduction-to-ai-for-work)

## **2. Easy-Vibe: AI Coding Guide from Scratch**

This guide is project-based and teaches you to build apps with AI tools. It covers vibe coding, frontend, backend, databases and deployment, and it also introduces AI agents and AI coding tools.

**Best for:** Beginners, founders, creators and product managers

**Link:** [Easy-Vibe (English version)](https://datawhalechina.github.io/easy-vibe/en/)

## **3. LLM Course (Maxime Labonne)**

This is a free roadmap for learning large language models. It runs from the fundamentals through fine-tuning, quantization, evaluation and deployment, and into LLM engineering. It gives you a clear path rather than scattered tutorials.

**Best for:** Developers, machine learning learners and AI builders

**Link:** [LLM Course on GitHub](https://github.com/mlabonne/llm-course)

## **4. LLM Zoomcamp (DataTalks.Club)**

This course is built around real-world LLM applications. You work through retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), vector search, embeddings, AI agents, function calling, evaluation, monitoring and reranking.

**Best for:** Software engineers, data engineers and machine learning learners who are ready to build

**Link:** [LLM Zoomcamp on GitHub](https://github.com/DataTalksClub/llm-zoomcamp)

## **5. Hugging Face LLM Course**

This course teaches LLMs and natural language processing using the Hugging Face ecosystem. You learn about Transformers, Datasets and Tokenizers, then move on to fine-tuning, sharing your models and more advanced LLM concepts.

**Best for:** AI developers and anyone interested in open-source LLMs

**Link:** [Hugging Face LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course/chapter1/1)





> #### **How to choose**
>
> Pick one course, not five. If AI is new to you, start with course 1. If you want to make something, try course 2. If you already code, choose between courses 3, 4 and 5 depending on whether you want a roadmap, a build-along or the open-source route. Whatever you choose, apply it to a real problem as you learn. That is what makes it stick.



***You don’t need a big budget to start learning AI. These five courses are free to begin, and together they cover the path from curiosity to building. Choose the one that matches where you are today, and give it a few focused weeks.***
