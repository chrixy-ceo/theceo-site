**About the opportunity:**



Skill Reset Nigeria is an initiative that aims to give Nigerians the practical digital skills needed to take part in the growing digital economy. Its target is to train one million people through free learning opportunities.



**Applicants pick one of six tracks:** 

* Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Automation
* Content Creation with AI
* Digital Marketing for Business
* Data Analysis with AI, Digital Productivity, and 
* Sales and Customer Experience. 



The skills are meant to support employment, entrepreneurship, business growth and career advancement. Participants who meet the programme requirements receive certification and can access job placement support.



It suits students, graduates, job seekers, business owners and working professionals who want to build in-demand digital skills without paying tuition fees. Choose the track that best fits your career goals or business needs.



**Who can apply:**



* Nigerians in all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory
* Students
* Graduates
* Job seekers
* Entrepreneurs
* Working professionals
* Anyone interested in improving their digital skills



What you get:

* Free digital skills training with no tuition fees
* Your choice of six learning tracks
* Career-relevant digital skills and knowledge
* Certification after meeting the programme requirements
* Job placement support
* A chance to improve your employability and business productivity
