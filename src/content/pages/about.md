## Hello, I’m Christabel

I am a pharmacist and a data and AI scientist. I analyse health and research data, automate repetitive work, and build tools that people actually use. This website brings together my work and two things I publish.

## What you will find here

- **Portfolio** (the home page): my projects, services, experience and the tools I use.
- **[Blog](/blog/)**: notes on data, automation, pharmacy and building.
- **[Infobytes](/infobytes/)**: a curated list of tech opportunities, scholarships, jobs and fellowships, with deadlines and how to apply.

## How Infobytes is put together

I look for opportunities from official and public sources, summarise the key details and link to the original page so you can apply directly. Infobytes is free to use. Please always check the details on the official page before you apply.
