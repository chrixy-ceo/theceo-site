## About the opportunity:

The National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, with funding from the Gates Foundation, has opened a mentorship programme for early-career women in vaccinology across Africa. Its aim is to help them grow professionally through structured mentoring, leadership development and a stronger network. This is a cohort of about 36 participants from Africa is paired with six mentors drawn from the United States and Africa. The year starts with a two-day in-person workshop. After that, participants have mentor meetings every two months and join monthly virtual training sessions. The content covers career development, leadership skills and sharing knowledge and experience with others in the field. For women early in their vaccine careers, it offers a ready-made community of peers and senior professionals, and the chance to build the kind of support network that can shape a career.



## Who can apply:

* Women in the early stage of their careers
* Currently working in vaccine development
* Living in a low- or middle-income country in Africa



## What you get:

* A two-day in-person workshop to kick off the programme
* Mentor meetings every two months
* Monthly virtual training sessions
* Leadership skills development
* Networking with vaccinology professionals in the US and Africa



## How to apply:

* Confirm that you meet all the eligibility criteria.

* Submit your application through the official portal between 15 October and 31 December 2026.
* Wait for the review, which runs from 1 January to 27 February 2027.
