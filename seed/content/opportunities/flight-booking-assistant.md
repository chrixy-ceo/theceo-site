**About the opportunity:**



FlyNaija is recruiting a Flight Booking Assistant to join its remote team. The role is aimed at people who enjoy helping others plan their journeys and want to start a career in travel and tourism.



Day to day, you would help customers make flight bookings and reservations, answer travel questions with accurate information, and explain the available flight options and booking details. You would also check customer information to make sure bookings are correct, guide customers to the right support when needed, and keep in good contact with clients throughout the process.



The role suits an organised, patient and detail-focused person with good English, a customer-friendly attitude and a reliable laptop and internet connection. It is open to people with 0 to 2 years of experience who can show the right skills and a willingness to learn.



**Who can apply:**

* Entry-level candidates with 0 to 2 years of experience
* Applicants with good written and spoken English
* Applicants with strong customer service and people skills
* People with an interest in flight bookings, travel arrangements and tourism
* Applicants with basic computer skills who are confident using a laptop
* Applicants with their own reliable laptop and stable internet connection
* Applicants with good organisation and time-management skills
* Applicants who pay close attention to detail, especially with passenger and booking information
* People with a professional, patient and helpful attitude



**What you get:**



* A monthly salary of ₦70,000
* A full-time, fully remote role
* A start in the travel and tourism industry
