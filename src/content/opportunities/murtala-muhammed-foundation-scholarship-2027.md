**About the opportunity:**



The Murtala Muhammed Foundation (MMF) runs this scholarship together with the Africa Science Academy to help young African women study Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics at university. Its aim is to remove the money barrier to higher education and help talented female students build the knowledge and skills to contribute to scientific and technological progress.



Successful applicants are supported through a four-year degree. Alongside the funding, they get practical work experience through paid internships each year, guidance from experienced mentors, and leadership development through an annual summer school. Applicants submit an online form with their transcripts, a passport photograph and essays.



It suits young women with a strong interest in STEM and a solid background in Physics, Mathematics, Further Mathematics and English who are about to begin university.



**Who can apply:**

* Female students from African countries
* Applicants aged between 16 and 19
* Applicants who have completed Senior High School, IGCSE, O-Level or an equivalent qualification
* Applicants who have studied Physics, Mathematics, Further Mathematics and English
* Applicants with a strong interest in Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics



**What you get:**



* Full funding for a four-year undergraduate degree
* Tuition coverage
* Accommodation support
* Living stipends
* Paid internship each year
* Mentorship from experienced mentors
* Annual leadership summer school
* No IELTS requirement





**How to apply:**



1. Read the eligibility requirements and application guidelines on the official portal.

2. Complete the application form on the portal.

3. Prepare your academic transcripts, a recent passport photograph and your essays.

4. If you can, ask a Mathematics or Science teacher for a recommendation letter. This is optional.

5. Submit your application and documents through the portal before the deadline.
