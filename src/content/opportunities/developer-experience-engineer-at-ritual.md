**About the opportunity:**



Ritual is building a blockchain designed from the start to support AI-native applications, and its private testnet is already live. Its first product, Infernet, lets developers use AI models both on-chain through smart contracts and off-chain.



As a Developer Experience Engineer, you would design and build the tools that make it easy for developers to build on Ritual. That includes development frameworks for AI-enabled chains similar in spirit to Foundry, fast local environments for testing, command-line tools and SDKs, and debugging and simulation tools for AI and blockchain interactions. You would also find the points where developers struggle and fix them, write clear documentation and tutorials, run workshops and hackathons, and help grow an open-source community around the tools.



The role suits an engineer who has built developer tools that people enjoy using, understands smart contracts and the EVM, and can explain technical ideas well in writing and in person. It also suits someone who takes full ownership of their work and is comfortable in a fast-moving environment.



**Who can apply:**



* Engineers with strong experience in at least one of Rust, TypeScript or Python
* Applicants who have contributed to open-source projects and libraries
* Applicants with a record of building developer tools that developers love to use
* Applicants with a deep understanding of EVM architecture and smart contract development
* Applicants with experience in modern AI development workflows and MLOps tools across different compute environments
* Applicants with strong knowledge of container technologies and deployment patterns
* Applicants who can explain complex technical ideas clearly through writing and speaking
* Applicants who take end-to-end ownership, communicate well and can meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment
* Maintaining or contributing to popular blockchain tools, building compiler toolchains, AI infrastructure experience, community-building and distributed systems knowledge are all advantages



**What you get:**



* A highly competitive compensation package
* An annual discretionary bonus
* Healthcare premiums fully covered
* Fully remote or hybrid working, your choice
* Virtual and in-person company events
