## **Who can apply:**

Researchers affiliated with a recognised higher education or research institution in Africa African scholars in the diaspora, as long as they partner with colleagues at an African-based institution. Individuals or teams of 3 to 5 people (interdisciplinary, multi-institution or multi-country teams are strongly encouraged)Women scholars, early-career academics and staff at less well-resourced universities are specifically encouraged to apply. Applicants with unfinished deliverables from earlier CODESRIA-funded projects are discouraged from applying

## What you get:

15 months of research support. Access to knowledge-exchange workshops and peer-learning forums. Opportunities to contribute to public dialogue and policy engagement. Award amount: Applicants submit a draft budget of USD 5,000 to USD 15,000)

## How to apply:

* Choose your focus: gender and associational life, or academic and intellectual freedom (studies linking the two are especially welcome).
* Prepare a recent CV (for teams, one for every member).
* Write a proposal synopsis of up to 4,000 words covering your research question, a review of recent literature, your conceptual framework, methodology and timeline, a draft budget and your planned outputs, both scholarly and non-scholarly. 
* Include a draft budget between USD 5,000 and USD 15,000.
* Get a formal letter of affiliation or endorsement from your institution. 
* Submit everything online through the CODESRIA application portal before the deadline (click the apply link below).



**Tip**: Prioritised sub-themes include digital transitions and AI in universities, university governance and funding models, and graduate outcomes, so tie your idea to one of them. Questions can go to tgf@codesria.org.
