**About the opportunity:**



The role is listed on OpenTrain, a platform where people find AI training and data-labelling projects. You would lead the visual direction and production of video and motion content used to evaluate AI systems, and review that content for technical accuracy, clarity and quality against project guidelines. You would work with brand, marketing, product and creative teams on launches and campaigns, turning complex AI and software ideas into clear, engaging visuals.



The work includes polished UI and product animations that show interfaces, dashboards, terminals, code and workflows, and experimental brand motion using particles, data effects, depth, 3D and generative elements. You would also develop visual concepts from first idea to final delivery.



The contract lasts 1 to 3 months. After you apply on OpenTrain, you finish your application on the hiring platform and take a short AI interview. Passing it is how you qualify for paid tasks. The role suits an experienced motion designer with a strong portfolio who can work independently and has a polished, technical, futuristic visual style.



**Who can apply:**



* Applicants based in Nigeria or another country on the listed eligible countries
* Applicants with professional fluency in English
* Applicants with a strong portfolio in motion design, product animation and technology-focused visuals
* Applicants with advanced After Effects skills and strong Figma experience
* Applicants with experience in Cinema 4D, Blender or similar 3D tools
* Applicants with strong editing skills in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or similar software
* Applicants who can develop concepts independently and take projects from idea to final delivery
* Experience with tools such as Rive, Lottie, Runway, Higgsfield, Kling, Midjourney, ComfyUI, Houdini, TouchDesigner, p5.js or Three.js is helpful



**What you get:**



* Pay of $43.27 to $72.12 per hour
* A remote contract of 1 to 3 months
* Flexible weekly hours



**How to apply:**



1. Apply on OpenTrain by creating a free account. You will then be sent to the hiring platform.

2. Complete your application on the hiring platform, where you will be asked for your resume, LinkedIn profile, phone number, location and answers to 4 screening questions.

3. Take the short AI interview soon after you are invited, since passing it is how you qualify for paid tasks.
