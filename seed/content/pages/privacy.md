## Overview

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this website, including the Blog and Infobytes sections, and how it is used. By using the site you agree to this policy.

## Information I collect

You do not need an account to read this site, and I do not ask you for personal details. If you contact me through WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn or X, I receive the information you choose to send and use it only to reply.

Like most websites, the hosting provider (Cloudflare) may automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to deliver the site securely and prevent abuse.

## Cookies and advertising

The Blog and Infobytes sections may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising in [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) or at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info). You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). The portfolio pages do not show ads.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

## Newsletter

Subscribing to the newsletter is optional. If you subscribe, your email address is collected and stored by the email service I use to send it, and is used only to send you updates from this site. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email.

## Calendar reminders

The Add to calendar buttons create a calendar entry on your own device or calendar account. Nothing is sent to or stored by this website.

## Local storage

Your choice of light or dark theme is saved in your browser so the site can remember it. It is not shared with anyone.

## Links to other sites

This site links to other websites, including WhatsApp, LinkedIn, X, GitHub and the organisations that publish the opportunities listed on Infobytes. I am not responsible for their privacy practices.

## Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. To make a request, use the [Contact](/contact/) page.

## Changes

I may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
