**About the opportunity:**



The TKA Foundation scholarship is for students who are preparing to enter a Nigerian public university or polytechnic in the 2027 academic session. The foundation gives priority to applicants from low-income families.



Registration closes at 11:59 PM Nigeria time on 20 December 2026. After that, the foundation runs a screening test on Saturday, 13 March 2027 at 9:00 AM Nigeria time. Selected students are sent the venue by email. Applicants should keep their application reference number, because confirmation and updates are sent to the email address they register with.



It suits school leavers who are preparing for JAMB and the 2027 admission process and need financial support to start their degree or diploma.



**Who can apply:**



* Students seeking admission to a Nigerian public university or polytechnic for the 2027 academic session
* Applicants with WAEC or an equivalent examination result from 2024 to 2027
* Applicants still waiting for their results, who may apply
* Applicants who are preparing for JAMB and the 2027 admission process
* Students from low-income families are given priority



**What you get:**



* Tuition fees
* Textbooks and stationery
* Bursary support
