**About the opportunity:**



Technology Talent Services (TTS) Nigeria is run by Tech4Dev, a nonprofit that uses technology education and digital skills to create opportunities for Africans, with funding from the Mastercard Foundation. The 18-month programme runs from February 2026 to July 2027 and is being rolled out across 12 states in Nigeria. This current call is for women in Lagos State.



The programme helps young women build in-demand digital and workplace skills and prepares them for jobs in the growing digital economy. It combines skills training with career preparation and pathways to employment, so participants gain skills that fit today's workplaces and not only classroom learning. Applicants choose from four pathways: cybersecurity, data and artificial intelligence, software engineering, and executive administrative assistant work with social media expertise. These cover both technical and non-coding careers.



It suits young women who are starting out in their careers and want to build employable skills. You do not need to have your whole career figured out before you apply. The programme especially encourages applications from women living with disabilities, internally displaced women and nursing mothers.



**Who can apply:**



* Women aged 18 to 35
* Women based in Lagos State
* Applicants who want to develop employable digital or tech-enabled skills
* Applicants who are willing to learn, grow and prepare for employment
* Applicants who can take part in the programme's training and development activities
* Young women living with disabilities, internally displaced women and nursing mothers are especially encouraged to apply



**What you get:**



* Free skills training
* Training in in-demand technology and digital career areas
* Practical workplace skills development
* Career preparation
* Pathways to employment opportunities
* Exposure to technology-enabled careers
