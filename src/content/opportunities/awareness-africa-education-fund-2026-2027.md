**About the opportunity:**



The Awareness Africa Education Fund is a need-based scholarship for a Nigerian student who is about to begin undergraduate studies at a federal or state university in the 2026/2027 academic session. It is meant for someone whose financial situation could stop them from starting university, or make it very hard to do so.



The fund contributes towards school fees and compulsory registration fees. Applicants were directed to an online application page, and the closing date was 31 October 2026. Applications for this round are now closed.



It suits students who have shown commitment to their studies, have a genuine financial need and are starting at a federal or state university. If you were planning to apply, check Awareness Africa's official channels for any later round.



**Who can apply:**



* Nigerian students starting undergraduate studies at a federal or state university in the 2026/2027 session
* Students who have shown academic commitment
* Students with a genuine financial need that could prevent or seriously hinder them from starting university



**What you get:**



* Financial support towards school fees
* Support towards compulsory registration fees
