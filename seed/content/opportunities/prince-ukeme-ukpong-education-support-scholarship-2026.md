**About the opportunity:**



The Prince Ukeme Ukpong Education Support Scholarship is run by the Prince Ukeme Ukpong Mega Foundation. It gives financial help to students who need support to continue their tertiary education and do well in their chosen fields.



The scholarship is linked to the foundation's yearly Learn, Excel and Empower with Prince Ukeme Ukpong programme, which takes place in Akwa Ibom State. Selected students receive their scholarship prizes at that event. The foundation stresses that applying is completely free and that it never asks for fees, agent charges or processing costs, and it asks anyone who gets such a request to report it to the foundation.



It suits Nigerian undergraduates from Akwa Ibom State who are already enrolled in a tertiary institution, need financial help and are committed to finishing their studies.



**Who can apply:**



* Nigerian citizens
* Students of Akwa Ibom State origin
* Students enrolled in a Nigerian tertiary institution
* Applicants who can show genuine financial need
* Applicants who are committed to continuing their education



**What you get:**



* A two-year scholarship
* Tuition coverage
* Presentation of the scholarship prize at the Learn, Excel and Empower with Prince Ukeme Ukpong programme
