**About the opportunity:**



The Anthony Udokwu Foundation offers this scholarship to help university students who face serious difficulties in continuing their education. The 2027/2028 edition is aimed at students whose circumstances meet the foundation's published eligibility criteria.



Those criteria cover students living with disabilities, orphans, students who have lost one parent or been abandoned and receive no meaningful support from the surviving parent, and students facing critical circumstances such as abuse, exploitation, serious neglect or abandonment that affect their education or safety. Successful applicants receive help with tuition fees and other academic support.



It suits university students in these situations who need financial backing to stay in school and complete their degrees.



**Who can apply:**



* Students living with disabilities
* Orphans (both parents deceased)
* Students who have lost one parent, or have been abandoned, with no meaningful support from the surviving parent
* Students facing critical extenuating circumstances, abuse, exploitation, serious neglect or abandonment that affect their education or safety



**What you get:**



* Tuition fee support
* Other academic support
