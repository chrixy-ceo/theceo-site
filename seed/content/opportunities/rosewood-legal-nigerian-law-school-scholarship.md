**About the opportunity:**



Rosewood Legal is a Nigerian law firm with experience in commercial law practice and alternative dispute resolution. Through this scholarship, the firm wants to support talented aspiring lawyers and help develop the next generation of legal professionals.



The 2027 scholarship backs law graduates as they begin their professional legal training at the Nigerian Law School. It is open to candidates who are scheduled to attend the First Quarter 2027 session, and the firm describes it as fully funded. The announcement does not list extra requirements such as a minimum grade, age limit or state of origin, so applicants should check the application form for any further conditions.



It suits Nigerian law graduates who have been admitted to, or are preparing for, the Law School's First Quarter 2027 session and need help with the costs.



**Who can apply:**



* Nigerians
* Prospective Nigerian Law School students
* Candidates scheduled to attend the First Quarter 2027 session of the Nigerian Law School



**What you get:**



* Law School fees
* Accommodation
* Feeding and study materials
