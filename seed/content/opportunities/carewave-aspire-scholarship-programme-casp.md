**About the opportunity:**



Care Wave Foundation is a registered non-profit organisation based in Abuja. Its work covers education, healthcare access, elder care, economic stability and support for underserved communities. The Carewave Aspire Scholarship Programme (CASP) is its merit- and need-based undergraduate scholarship, designed especially for students from vulnerable or financially disadvantaged communities.



The initial award covers one academic year and is worth ₦500,000. It is meant to help with education and living costs, and the foundation's guidance says this may include tuition fees, books, transport and upkeep. Renewal is not automatic. Continued support depends on academic performance, meeting the programme's requirements and staying in good academic standing, and recipients may need to send progress reports every term.



Applications are made online through the foundation's scholarship portal and include a motivation letter of 600 to 1,000 words. Applications are reviewed and verified, and shortlisted candidates may be invited to an interview, either online or in person. The scholarship suits students with strong academic potential, real financial need, integrity and leadership potential, and a clear commitment to finishing their degree.



**Who can apply:**



* Nigerian citizens
* Students who have secured admission into an eligible Federal University in Nigeria
* Applicants for undergraduate study
* Students with strong academic performance
* Students who can show genuine financial need
* Students from vulnerable or financially disadvantaged communities
* Applicants who show integrity, leadership potential and commitment to their education
* Applicants who can write a motivation letter of 600 to 1,000 words



**What you get:**



* An annual award of ₦500,000
* Initial support that covers one academic year
* Support for educational and living-related costs
* Possible coverage of tuition fees, books, transport and upkeep, depending on the approved support
* Consideration for renewal in later academic sessions, based on performance and compliance
* No IELTS requirement
