**About the opportunity:**

BuzzyBooth.com is recruiting a creator who can turn ideas into finished, scroll-stopping videos using AI generation tools. The company says it wants someone who can take a project all the way from concept to final edit, rather than someone who only writes prompts or produces random AI clips.



Day to day, you would make video ads and short-form content for platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. That includes developing concepts, hooks, scripts and storyboards, then combining AI-generated footage with real footage, voiceovers, sound effects, captions and music. You would also produce several versions of the same ad to test different hooks and messaging, and keep up with new AI video tools and social media trends.



The role suits someone who loves experimenting with new tools, understands what makes a viewer keep watching in the first few seconds, and can produce several high-quality videos each week without constant direction.



**Who can apply:**

* Mid-level creators with proven experience making AI-generated video ads or social media Reels
* Applicants with a strong portfolio of real AI video projects they have produced
* Applicants experienced with AI video tools such as Google Veo, Kling AI, Runway, Sora, Higgsfield or similar
* Applicants with solid editing skills in CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects or comparable software
* Creators who can make realistic AI videos that look polished and natural
* Applicants who understand hooks, storytelling, pacing and audience retention
* People who can work independently, take feedback well and deliver consistently
