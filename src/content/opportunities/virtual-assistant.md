**About the opportunity:**



Turning Teen is looking for a reliable, organised Virtual Assistant to help keep daily operations running smoothly. The role is full-time and fully remote, and it suits someone at junior level.



The work mixes creative and administrative tasks. You would design engaging social media graphics in Canva, reply professionally to routine email enquiries, and help coordinate and schedule classes using Acuity Scheduling. You would also give general admin support and make sure your assigned tasks are finished accurately and on time. In doing this, you would support the organisation's online presence and its communication with clients.



It suits someone who is comfortable with Canva, writes clearly, manages their time well and pays close attention to detail.



**Who can apply:**

* Junior-level professionals with experience using Canva for graphic design
* People who can create graphics for social media platforms
* Applicants with good written communication skills who can handle email enquiries
* Applicants with strong organisation and time-management skills
* Applicants with excellent communication and attention to detail
* Familiarity with scheduling platforms such as Acuity Scheduling is an added advantage



**How to apply:**



1. Prepare your CV.

2. Put together a portfolio, ideally with samples of your previous graphic design or social media work.

3. Email your CV and portfolio to the address behind the Apply button on this page before the deadline.
